MAMA: gentle workouts after birth

Low-impact moves to rebuild your core, pelvic floor and strength. Start only when your doctor or midwife says you are ready. Timing differs for everyone: often around 6 weeks after a vaginal birth, and often 8 to 12 weeks or more after a C-section.

There are no crunches, sit-ups or planks here. Go slowly. Stop and speak to your doctor if you feel pain, pulling at a C-section scar, heaviness or pressure in your pelvis, leaking, a bulge along your belly, dizziness, or if bleeding increases or returns.

These moves strengthen your core. They cannot remove belly fat from one spot. Walking, good food, sleep and time all help too.

                      How EMOM works

                      EMOM = Every Minute On the Minute.

                      1. Each workout has four exercises.
                      2. Every minute, one exercise starts. Do the reps right away.
                      3. Rest for whatever time is left in that minute.
                      4. Four minutes = one round. Repeat for 3, 4 or 5 rounds.

                      New to training? Start with 12 minutes. Add rounds only when your form and recovery feel good.
